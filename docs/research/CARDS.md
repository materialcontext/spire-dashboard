# Card dossier

Every card in the Slay the Spire 2 beta (Spire Codex data, v0.113.1): what it does, what the deck simulator reads from it, and what it would take to read the rest. Written for the simulator overhaul; [INTERACTIONS.md](INTERACTIONS.md) builds on it, and [RELICS.md](RELICS.md) does the same for relics.

Card text and numbers come from the Codex card data (`cards.json`, with `description_raw`, vars and upgrades). Keyword and power rules come from the Codex keyword, glossary and power definitions. Where the data is silent, a note says so; two facts come from community guides instead, and are marked where used (how Melodies are gained, and that Strength does not apply to Osty). The simulator reading is generated by `cargo run -p spire-data --example audit -- --coverage` after the simulator overhaul. The notes were written before it: they describe each gap as it was, and a struck-through code (~~`CODE`~~) is one the overhaul closed.

## How to read an entry

```
**Name** · cost · type · rarity · keywords · coverage
Card text.
*Upgraded:* upgraded text, or what the upgrade changes.
*Makes:* cards it creates.
*Simulator:* what `play_of` and the mechanics reader take from it.
*Unread:* trigger sentences the reader leaves out.
Note on how it works and what is missing. `GAP_CODES`
```

Coverage, after the overhaul: ✅ read (everything that matters to the fight output is modelled), ◐ partly read, ○ not read (the card does nothing in the simulator), ⚠️ misread (the simulator credits something the card doesn't do, or drops what it does do in a way that changes today's numbers). Gap codes are listed in [Gaps](#gaps), grouped by the simulator change that would close them.

### What the overhaul changed

The overhaul worked through the [Gaps](#gaps) family by family. 17 of the 19 reader-bug causes are closed. The two left are Mad Science, whose effect depends on an event choice, and Heavenly Drill, whose X isn't doubled at 4 or more. What it added:

- **Triggers.** Applying a debuff, creating a card (or a named one, or a Status), gaining or spending Stars, evokes, gaining a Melody (or every Melody), losing HP, discards, dislodges, shots, Osty attacks, drawing a keyword or type, "every N" counters, and "the first time each turn or combat".
- **This turn and next turn.** Temporary Strength, Dexterity and Focus; triggers that last a turn; cost changes (cheaper per X, next card free, a capped or free hand, everything costs more); kept hands, draw locks, play limits, Block locks; double energy and double Block; and a queue for effects that arrive in later turns (draws, Block, channels, Stars, damage).
- **Conditions and counts.** A card can be gated on tracked state, or get a bonus from it: Vulnerable, Poison, Impaled, an Arrow ready, the piles, the hand, Doom applied this turn, Melodies held, Osty alive. Its damage, hits, Block or draw can scale with a count: Block, orbs, hand, piles, enemy debuffs, cards drawn, played, made or Ethereal, Attacks, Skills, energy and Stars this turn, deck cards by name or cost, Osty attacks and Osty HP.
- **Per-card state.** Growth per play or per draw (by card or by name), its own cost going up or down, and named cards doubled.
- **Choices.** Discard, exhaust, fetch and autoplay with a policy: discard or exhaust the least wanted card, Sly cards first; fetch and autoplay the best one for the deck's lean. A random card is drawn from an even spread of up to 12 cards from the named pool (Common to Rare, by id), and rarity weights are ignored.
- **Character systems.** Evoke on demand, orb passives on demand, Osty's HP, Doom as enemy state, and Melodies.
- **Survival.** Enemy Weak lowers incoming damage (Weak's percentage comes from the power data), and your own HP loss is counted. Enchantments on deck cards are applied.

Assumptions where the data is silent. Each one is a single place in `sim.rs`:

- Playing a Wah, Cho or Ki card gains that Melody, and Melodies last the whole fight (community guides).
- Osty's HP is the sum of Summons. He takes no damage from enemies, and Strength doesn't apply to his attacks (community guides).
- Block carried over by Barricade, Blur or Sturdy Clamp isn't modelled.
- The data doesn't mark which cards are Tarot cards, so cards that make random Tarot cards make nothing.

Open misreads: Hailstorm drops its Frost condition, so it overcredits. Heavenly Drill undercounts, and Mad Science applies both event variants.

## Rules the cards rely on

From the Codex glossary, keyword and power definitions unless marked otherwise.

**Turn.** You draw 5 cards at the start of each turn. Energy pays for cards (3 per turn for every character). Block prevents damage until your next turn. When the Draw Pile is empty, the Discard Pile is shuffled into it.

**Keywords.**

- Exhaust: removed until the end of combat.
- Ethereal: exhausted if still in the hand at end of turn.
- Retain: not discarded at end of turn.
- Innate: starts each combat in the hand.
- Sly: if discarded from the hand before the end of your turn, it is played for free.
- Unplayable: can't be played.
- Eternal: can't be removed or transformed from the deck.
- Replay: plays the card an additional time.
- Fatal: triggers when the card kills a non-minion enemy.
- Transform: the card becomes a random card of any rarity, unless the text names one.
- Stun: the enemy doesn't act on its next turn.

**Buffs and debuffs.**

- Strength: + attack damage per hit.
- Dexterity: + Block from cards.
- Focus: + orb effects (not Plasma).
- Vigor: the next Attack deals that much more.
- Vulnerable: +50% attack damage taken, for N turns.
- Weak: −25% attack damage dealt.
- Frail: −25% Block from cards.
- Poison: lose that much HP at the start of the creature's turn, then 1 less.
- Doom: at the end of the enemy turn, the creature dies if its HP is at or below its Doom.
- Plating: Block at end of turn, −1 each turn.
- Thorns: damage back when hit by an attack.
- Intangible: all damage and HP loss reduced to 1 this turn.
- Buffer: prevents the next HP loss.
- Artifact: negates debuffs.

**Character systems.**

- *Defect, orbs (3 slots):*
  - Channelling fills the first empty slot.
  - With no slot free, the first orb is evoked to make room.
  - Evoke consumes the rightmost orb, unless the card says leftmost.
  - The orbs, with passive and then evoke values:

    | Orb | Passive | Evoke |
    |---|---|---|
    | Lightning | 3 to a random enemy at end of turn | 8 |
    | Frost | 2 Block at end of turn | 5 Block |
    | Dark | gains 6 each end of turn | its stored damage, to the lowest-HP enemy |
    | Glass | 4 to all enemies at end of turn, then loses 1 | 8 to all |
    | Plasma | 1 energy at start of turn | 2 energy; ignores Focus |
- *Necrobinder, Osty:*
  - Summon N summons Osty, or raises his Max HP for this combat if he is already up.
  - Bound Phylactery (the starting relic) summons 1 each turn.
  - Osty attacks ("Osty deals") are his, not yours. Community guides say Strength doesn't apply to them; the Codex data doesn't say.
  - Souls are 0-cost Exhaust Skills that draw 2.
- *Regent, Stars and Forge:*
  - Stars are a second resource that some cards cost (shown as "+ N Stars").
  - The first Forge each combat adds Sovereign Blade (2 energy, 10 damage, Retain) to the hand. Each Forge adds its amount to the Blade's damage.
- *The Fool, Arrows (1 slot):*
  - Cards that "Shoot an Arrow" spend an Arrow, which sticks in the enemy (Impaled).
  - An Impaled enemy's Arrow is Dislodged (returned) after you hit it 3 more times, or by a card that Dislodges.
  - Aim adds damage to your next Arrow; Song Bow (the starting relic) starts each combat with 6 Aim.
  - Stretch adds Block to your next Block gain from a card.
- *The Fool, Melodies:*
  - Wah, Cho and Ki Melodies are what Melody payoffs count ("each Melody you have", "when you gain every Melody").
  - The data doesn't say how a Melody is gained. Community guides say playing a Wah, Cho or Ki card gains that Melody.
- *The Fool, other cards:*
  - Sadness is a Retain, Unplayable Status that Fool cards make; Growth, Punch of Joy and Ecstasy turn it into value.
  - Joy is a 0-cost Exhaust Skill: +1 energy, draw 1.
  - Mythic Form makes random Tarot cards (22 tokens).

**What the simulator models.** It plays seeded fights against a passive target:

- It shuffles, draws 5, and spends energy greedily under two policies: one leaning to damage, one to Block.
- It carries Strength, Dexterity, Focus, orbs, Poison, Plating, Vulnerable, Weak, Doom, Aim, Stretch, Vigor, Stars, Arrows, Forge, Melodies, Osty's HP and the piles between turns, and counts your HP loss.
- Powers and relics run through the mechanics reader's triggers. Cards carry conditions, counts, per-card growth and cost changes, and effects queued for later turns.
- Choices (discard, exhaust, fetch, autoplay) follow a policy, and random cards come from a spread of the named pool.
- It has no enemy attacks, no enemy HP and no kills, and no potions. Survival is a separate check (`survival.rs`) against the enemy's turn patterns, which takes the simulator's enemy Weak and your self-damage into account.

## Tag reader errors

Tags feed roles, limits and the composition measures, so these matter beyond the simulator. They were found by checking every card's tags against its text, and all five groups are fixed:

- **"Weak" from your own Strength loss.** The Weak rule now needs an enemy subject ("enemy loses", "enemies lose", "it loses" … Strength). Mangle and Piercing Wail keep Weak; Brand, Bulk Up, Friendship and Rupture lose it.
- **"Energy N" from a cost or threshold.** The Energy tag needs a "Gain N Energy" sentence. Banshee's Cry, Danse Macabre, Expect a Fight, Melancholy, Right Hand Hand, Transfigure and Tune lose it.
- **Effects on another player.** Ally-targeted cards (Believe in You, Constellation) get no tags.
- **Statuses tagged by their drawback.** Status and Curse cards get no tags.
- **Misread powers.** The powers read once on play now read per event, so their tags come from the right timing. Caltrops is still tagged as damage rather than chip, because Thorns needs enemy attacks.

`audit --coverage` prints each card's tags in its last column.
